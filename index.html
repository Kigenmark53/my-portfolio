02 About

Curious by default,
shipping by habit.

I’m a software development student at KCA University in Nairobi. Most of what I know came from building things that broke — a store that lost its session, a database that needed normalising, a game loop that ran too fast.

I like the unglamorous middle of software: modelling the data properly, wiring the API, handling the error case nobody thought about. And I like finishing — a half-built system teaches you nothing.

Right now I’m sharpening JavaScript and TypeScript, going deeper on backend architecture, and looking for a team where I can learn from people who have shipped more than I have.
